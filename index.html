A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Do you take on residential and commercial projects?

Yes. J & M Contracting specializes in both residential and commercial concrete placement, site work, and dirt work.

Can my existing concrete be resurfaced?

The team offers resurfacing for concrete driveways, patios, sidewalks, and more. Contact J & M to discuss your existing surface and whether resurfacing fits your project.

Where are you based?

J & M Contracting is based in Hewitt, Texas. Share your project location with the team to confirm availability in your area.

What should I include in my inquiry?

Your project location, the work you need, approximate dimensions, and your preferred timing are a helpful start. You can attach photos when you send the prepared email.
